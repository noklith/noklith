The details matter. Here are the things to know about the account, the card, and the lines between them.

OPEN YOUR ACCOUNT
What is Noklith?

Noklith is a self-custodial money app designed around your account, named Slabs, and a card for everyday spending. Explore your account, Slabs, card, and rules from one dashboard.

Who controls the money in my account?

You do, through your own wallet. Noklith never holds your keys. Money you move onto a card is held by that card program and is no longer in your keys.

How does Float work?

You choose a target card balance, a refill threshold, and a daily cap. Where the card program supports a balance feed, Float can refill within your signed limits. You can revoke the automation. Where a feature is unavailable, manual loads are used instead.

Does the card need an ID check?

The proposed no-ID card option applies only within the card program’s limits and eligible regions. Limits can be lower by region and can change. Higher limits and future metal cards follow the issuer’s own onboarding. Card availability is subject to program confirmation.

What are Slabs and payday rules?

Slabs are named balances inside your account. Payday rules divide incoming money between your Slabs using the proportions you set. Moves into Park or Stocks require your signature in the initial product design.

What happens if a card load fails?

Your transfer is checked on chain first. If the card program cannot take the load right away, it stays pending and is retried. It is never credited twice, and a load that keeps failing is reviewed by the operator.

Which networks are in the plan?

Base is the home network. The product plan includes same-address accounts on Arbitrum, BNB Chain, Ethereum, and Robinhood Chain, plus a Solana deposit route. Deployment and route availability must be confirmed as each stage launches.

Can I try the app here?

The dashboard brings your account, Slabs, card, and rules together. Connect your account to access financial actions. Until then, explore the interactive guides to see how the product works.
